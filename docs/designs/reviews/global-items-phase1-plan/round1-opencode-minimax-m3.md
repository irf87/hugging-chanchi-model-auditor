## What must not change

- **The design is the spec.** Nothing in this report asks for a design change; R1-04 resolves to
  making the SQL match the design's stated last-segment rule, not to changing the design.
- **The design's outcome table and the owner's approval rule** (`pass_with_fixes` with no blocking
  and no major = an approval). R1-01 must not be "fixed" by weakening the rule to make the numbers
  work.
- **The three hand-run rounds and their recorded outcomes** in `docs/designs/reviews/global-items/`.
  R1-01 pins the numbers in the plan; it must not edit the reviews, which are history.
- **The failure-closed rule**: an unknown family never counts as a distinct family and a round that
  relies on one is degraded. Do not relax it to make a round non-degraded.
- **The privacy rule**: no home path and no reference-project name in any committed file. R1-02's fix
  must not add a real home path to the plan.
- **The session protocol's explore budget** (`max(1, floor(n/4))`, no explore on blocking work) and
  the rule that executors never commit, merge or write chanchi records.
- **The D7 test itself** (SQL and Python must agree). R1-04 strengthens it; it must not be dropped
  in favour of "the SQL is right".
